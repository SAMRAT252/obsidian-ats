About ATS System

The Problem

Modern recruitment heavily relies on Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) that often filter out qualified candidates simply because their resumes aren't perfectly formatted or lack specific keywords.

Our Solution

We provide a premium AI-powered tool to analyze your resume against job descriptions, using advanced NLP and vector similarity to give you actionable insights.

How It Works

Our system parses your PDF/DOCX resume, extracts text, tokenizes it, and calculates semantic similarity against the job description using TF-IDF and keyword matching.

Architecture Overview

User Upload -> Resume Parsing (PyPDF2/python-docx) 
-> Text Processing (NLTK) 
-> Skill Extraction 
-> Vectorization (TF-IDF) 
-> ATS Score Calculation
-> Recommendation Engine

Future Scope

In the future, we plan to add generative AI suggestions for rewriting specific bullet points to maximize ATS compatibility.
